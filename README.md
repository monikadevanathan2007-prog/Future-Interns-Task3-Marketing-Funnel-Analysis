# Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Internship (Task 3)

### Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing marketing campaign performance and customer conversion behavior using Power BI. The objective was to identify factors influencing customer conversions, evaluate campaign effectiveness, and develop interactive dashboards that support data-driven decision-making.

The analysis was performed using the Bank Marketing Dataset, which contains customer demographics, communication methods, loan information, and campaign outcomes.

---

## Project Objectives

* Analyze overall marketing campaign performance.
* Calculate customer conversion rates.
* Identify high-performing communication channels.
* Examine conversion trends across different months.
* Analyze customer segments based on job, education, marital status, and loan information.
* Create interactive dashboards for business intelligence and decision-making.

## Key Findings

### Conversion Performance

* Total Contacts: 5,000
* Converted Customers: 521
* Conversion Rate: 11.52%

### Contact Method Analysis

* Cellular communication generated the highest number of successful conversions.
* Telephone and unknown contact methods produced lower conversion results.

### Monthly Trend Analysis

* Conversion performance varied across different months.
* Certain months generated significantly higher customer engagement.

### Job Category Analysis

* Management and technician occupations contributed the highest number of conversions.
* Professional customer segments demonstrated stronger conversion behavior.

### Education Analysis

* Customers with secondary and tertiary education levels represented the largest share of successful conversions.

### Customer Segmentation Analysis

* Marital status, housing loans, and personal loans influenced customer conversion behavior.
* Interactive filters allowed deeper exploration of customer groups.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are suggested:

1. Increase the use of cellular communication channels for future marketing campaigns.
2. Focus marketing efforts on high-performing customer segments.
3. Develop personalized campaigns for professional occupations.
4. Optimize campaign timing using monthly conversion trends.
5. Use customer segmentation strategies to improve conversion rates and marketing efficiency.
